WebP to PNG / JPG — why it matters

WebP is great for the web, but many apps (Photoshop, older software, some upload forms) still demand PNG or JPG. This converter changes formats without uploading anything — the #1 searched image conversion task online.

Which format should I choose?

  • WebP — smallest files, best for websites.
  • JPG — universal compatibility for photos.
  • PNG — lossless, keeps transparency; bigger files.

FAQs

Does converting WebP to JPG lose quality?

Both are lossy formats, so one re-encode at quality 90+ is visually identical.

Can I convert HEIC (iPhone photos)?

Browser support for HEIC is limited — convert HEIC to JPG on your phone first, then use this tool freely.
