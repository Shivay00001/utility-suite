Why use browser-based tools?

Most free online tools upload your files to a server. VisionQuantech Tools is different: every tool runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript. That means your photos, PDFs and documents are never sent anywhere — they stay on your device. It's faster (no upload wait), private, and works offline once the page loads.
